About

tramaj is written by Lucas DiCioccio, who also writes about it, and about the other tools it grows alongside, on his blog at dicioccio.fr. The source is on GitHub.

Related sites

  • salmon — a Haskell toolkit for describing infrastructure as a graph of idempotent operations. Its site is built with Kitchen Sink too.
  • agents-exe — a runner for LLM agents, with a Kitchen Sink site as well.
  • dicioccio.fr — the author’s blog, also built with Kitchen Sink.
